## Reflection



### 1. What went well during this unit?

We were able to decide on a clear idea for our final project, studypot, and outline what the app should do before starting development. Writing the user stories helped us think through how students would browse, create, and join study sessions. We also identified features that fit the course requirements, which gives us a good starting point for the next milestones.

### 2. What were some challenges your group faced in this unit?

One challenge was choosing features that would be useful without making the app too complicated to finish. We had to think about how study sessions and participants would be connected in the database, while also making sure we could cover the required baseline features and two custom features. Breaking the idea down into smaller user stories helped us keep the scope manageable.

### 3. What additional support will you need in upcoming units as you continue to work on your final project?

It would help to get feedback on our database design, especially the relationships between students, study sessions, and participants. We may also need guidance when connecting the React frontend to the Express API and deploying the completed app on Render. Examples of organizing a full-stack project and debugging deployment issues would be useful as we build studypot.
