## Reflection

### 1. What went well during this unit?

One of the biggest things that went well was creating a team group chat on iMessage. It helped us a lot with coordinating tasks and working together. We also figured out how to use issues in GitHub: we created six issues, and all of them have been completed. Because of this, we got the core of studypot working, including the dashboard, search and filters, the create session modal, and the session details page with join and leave. We are now on track to finish the project earlier than we planned.

### 2. What were some challenges your group faced in this unit?

Creating the project board and organizing our work in it was a challenge, since it was our first time doing it. Communication was also hard at first. We started on Slack, but because of how notifications worked, we were not able to connect with each other well and we were all out of sync. We overcame this by moving to our iMessage group chat, and now we are able to stay on the same page.

### Did you finish all of your tasks in your sprint plan for this week? If you did not finish all of the planned tasks, how would you prioritize the remaining tasks on your list?

We finished most of our planned tasks. We still need to finish editing and deleting sessions, and we also want to improve the UI to make it friendlier for students. Our first priority is finishing edit and delete sessions, and then we will work on the UI changes.

### Which features and user stories would you consider “at risk”? How will you change your plan if those items remain “at risk”?

Edit and Delete Sessions is the main feature at risk because the backend is done but the frontend is still in progress. If it stays at risk, we will focus on it first as a team and keep it simple, such as adding edit and delete options on the session details page, before moving on to the UI improvements.

### 5. What additional support will you need in upcoming units as you continue to work on your final project?

The slides were helpful when we were creating issues and our project board for the first time. Going forward, we want to keep making progress as a team and try to finish each milestone faster. We do not think we need any additional support right now.
