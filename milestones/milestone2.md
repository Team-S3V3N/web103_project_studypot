## Reflection

### 1. What went well during this unit?

Our group made good progress turning the studypot idea into a clear design. We planned three main pages—Discover Sessions, Session Details, and My Sessions—plus a modal for creating study sessions. We also mapped out the database tables and their relationships, which gives us a good starting point for development.

### 2. What were some challenges your group faced in this unit?

One challenge was making sure the presentation, wireframes, and GitHub planning files all describe the same app. We also had to keep the feature list realistic for the time available, especially when deciding how joining sessions and managing created sessions should work.

### 3. What additional support will you need in upcoming units as you continue to work on your final project?

Feedback on our wireframes and database design would be helpful before we start coding. We may also need guidance with building the REST API for joining and leaving sessions, connecting our React frontend to PostgreSQL through Express, and deploying the finished app on Render.
